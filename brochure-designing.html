Brochure Designing

Tell your business story with clarity

Discuss your project ↗
DESIGNED AROUND YOUR MESSAGE

Tell your business story with clarity

A brochure gives prospects a guided introduction to your products or services. We shape the reading order so people can understand your offer, compare information and find the next step without feeling overwhelmed.

Who it is for

Company profiles, product launches, service introductions and sales presentations.

Start with a clear brief

Share your objective, audience, brand assets, intended formats and timeline. We will agree the scope, deliverables and review stages before work begins.

Discuss your requirements →

What the service includes

  • Plan a page or panel structure around audience questions and your objective.
  • Combine supplied copy, photography, headings and calls to action.
  • Review folds, pagination, image quality and print specifications.

What you receive

  • A layout plan and agreed creative direction.
  • Approved brochure artwork for the contracted page or panel count.
  • Print-ready PDF and a screen-friendly PDF when included.
A CLEAR PATH FROM BRIEF TO HANDOVER

How your project takes shape

  1. 01

    Understand

    Discuss the audience, message, formats and practical requirements. Confirm deliverables and responsibilities.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Develop the agreed creative direction and present the concept or layout for your feedback.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Consolidate your feedback, complete the contracted revisions and review the final details together.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Prepare the approved files for their intended use and confirm the handover requirements.

Agree the details before production

Copywriting, photography, translation and printing can be scoped separately.

Questions about brochure designing

How are the cost and timeline decided?

The quote depends on the brief, number of concepts or pages, formats, asset readiness and review schedule. We confirm a timeline after agreeing the scope.

Can you match our existing brand?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines, logo files and approved examples so the design can follow your established identity. Any proposed changes are discussed before approval.

Are editable files and revisions included?

The proposal specifies the included revision rounds, final formats and editable source files. Additional variants or changes after approval are quoted separately.

Tell us what you want to create.

Share your brief and intended use so we can recommend a practical next step.
