A Transparent Process from Planning to Delivery

Clear expectations and regular communication help advertising projects run more smoothly. Our proposed workflow keeps objectives, creative approvals, deliverables, and next steps visible.

Start a Conversation ↗

A Clear Process for Every Project

Every advertising project has different requirements. A structured workflow helps clarify what needs to be done, who needs to approve it, and how the work progresses from the initial discussion to final delivery.

01

Discovery and Discussion

Discuss your business, campaign objectives, target audience, budget, preferred channels, and expected deliverables.

02

Proposal and Planning

Define the proposed scope, deliverables, schedule, costs, and responsibilities for the project.

03

Creative Development

Develop the agreed advertising concepts, designs, and campaign materials for review.

04

Review and Approval

Collect feedback, confirm revisions, and obtain the required approvals before proceeding to execution.

05

Execution and Delivery

Prepare and deliver approved materials according to the agreed scope and project schedule.

06

Review and Next Steps

Where campaign data is available, review results and discuss potential improvements for future activities.

Communication You Can Follow

Clear project updates, documented decisions, and agreed approval stages help everyone understand the current status and the next steps. Specific timelines and reporting arrangements should be agreed upon for each project.
