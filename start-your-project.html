YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

Bring your ideas. We’ll ask the right questions.

Use these prompts to prepare. It is fine if you do not have every answer yet.

01

What would you like to change?

More relevant enquiries, a clearer brand or a stronger launch? Explain the main problem and what progress would look like.

02

Who are we speaking to?

Share your audience, locations, customer needs and the questions people ask before choosing your business.

03

What is already in place?

Bring your website link, brand assets, past campaign learnings and examples of a style you like.

04

What are the boundaries?

Share your target launch date, working budget and who will review the project so the scope stays realistic.

CLARITY BEFORE COMMITMENT

Know what the proposal should cover.

Agree these details in writing before approving the work.

  • Scope & deliverablesWhat is included, the formats required and the boundaries of the project.
  • Budget & media spendUnderstand service fees, advertising spend and third-party costs separately.
  • Milestones & approvalsConfirm timelines, review stages, revision allowances and who signs off.
  • Measurement & handoverAgree reporting, success measures and the assets or access to be handed over.
BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few useful answers.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Start with your business goal and what is getting in the way. The right mix of services can be discussed from there.

How much will my project cost?

Cost depends on scope, creative requirements, channels and timing. Request a tailored proposal and confirm whether media spend and external production costs are included.

How soon can a campaign go live?

Timing depends on the brief, asset availability, approvals and platform requirements. Agree a realistic schedule after the scope is reviewed.

Can we begin with one service?

You can enquire about a focused piece of work such as branding, a website or a campaign. Discuss priorities first so the proposed scope matches your needs.

What should we measure?

Choose measures connected to your goal, such as relevant enquiries, sales or qualified website visits. Results depend on the offer, market, budget and execution; campaign outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Ready to talk it through?

Share your goal, your website and any timing you have in mind.
