Festival Greetings for Your Business

A festival greeting should feel warm, relevant and respectful of the occasion. We create branded greetings that bring together thoughtful messages, appropriate imagery and a measured use of your identity. Designs can be planned in advance so your team has approved artwork ready for important dates.

Who this service is for

Festival wishes, national occasions, seasonal greetings, customer appreciation and employee communication.

Start with a clear brief

Share your objective, audience, brand assets, intended formats and timeline. We will agree the scope, deliverables and review stages before work begins.

How Festival Greetings Supports Your Brand

Connect with a personal message

A considered greeting helps your business acknowledge the occasions that matter to your audience.

Keep your brand present

Subtle logo placement and consistent styling make the greeting recognisable without overwhelming its sentiment.

Plan ahead

An agreed calendar and review schedule reduce last-minute artwork changes around busy occasions.

KNOW WHAT YOUR PROJECT INCLUDES

Our Festival Greetings Service

Scope of work

  • Occasion-specific visual concepts with appropriate motifs and colour choices.
  • Placement of approved greeting text, logo and optional sender details.
  • Social, messaging, email or print formats as agreed.
  • Language and branch variants in the contracted quantities.

Deliverables and formats

Receive approved greeting artwork in the selected formats and sizes. Animation, translation, custom illustration, print production and personalised bulk variants require an agreed scope.

Your proposal confirms quantities, revision rounds, responsibilities and delivery dates before work begins.

Our Process

  1. 01

    Schedule

    Agree the occasions, audience and delivery calendar.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Choose a suitable message, imagery and brand treatment.

  3. 03

    Approve

    Review names, translations, dates and cultural appropriateness.

  4. 04

    Share

    Supply approved versions ready for the agreed channels.

Why Work with Global Advertising Media?

We connect the design brief to the way your business will use the finished work. You review the creative direction at agreed stages, share consolidated feedback and approve the final details before handover. Clear scope and organised files help keep the project practical for your team.

Questions About Festival Greetings

Can you prepare a full festival calendar?

Yes. We can agree an advance list of occasions and a delivery schedule. Your team approves the relevant dates and messages.

Can greetings be made in regional languages?

Yes, using approved text and suitable fonts. Translation and language proofreading must be assigned and scoped before production.

Can you create animated greetings?

Yes. Motion, duration, audio and export formats need a separate agreed brief.

Ready to plan your festival greetings project?

Tell us your objective, intended use and timeline so we can recommend a suitable scope.
