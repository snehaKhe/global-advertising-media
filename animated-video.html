Animated Video for Your Business

Animation helps explain ideas that are difficult to show in a single image. We shape your message into a clear narrative, then use illustration, typography, movement and sound to guide the viewer. The production approach is chosen around your audience, distribution channels and available budget.

Who this service is for

Service explainers, product introductions, training content, brand stories, promotional motion graphics and presentation videos.

Start with a clear brief

Share your objective, audience, brand assets, intended formats and timeline. We will agree the scope, deliverables and review stages before work begins.

Discuss your requirements →

How Animated Video Supports Your Brand

Make complex ideas easier to follow

A planned sequence can break a service, process or product story into manageable steps.

Create a consistent visual world

Custom graphics and a defined motion style help the video feel connected to your brand.

Adapt the story for different channels

Agreed cutdowns, subtitles and aspect ratios help the same idea work in presentations and social placements.

KNOW WHAT YOUR PROJECT INCLUDES

Our Animated Video Service

Scope of work

  • Message development and script preparation or refinement as agreed.
  • Storyboard, style frames and visual direction before full animation.
  • Animation, editing and approved audio integration within the contracted scope.
  • Subtitle, duration and platform export checks for agreed versions.

Deliverables and formats

Deliverables may include an approved script and storyboard, a finished video master and contracted cutdowns or subtitle files. Voice-over, music licences, 3D production and editable project files must be specified upfront.

Your proposal confirms quantities, revision rounds, responsibilities and delivery dates before work begins.

Our Process

  1. 01

    Script

    Build a focused narrative around the viewer’s needs and next step.

  2. 02

    Storyboard

    Agree scene order, visual style and key frames.

  3. 03

    Animate

    Produce movement, timing, transitions and approved sound.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Review subtitles, audio levels and final exports.

Why Work with Global Advertising Media?

We connect the design brief to the way your business will use the finished work. You review the creative direction at agreed stages, share consolidated feedback and approve the final details before handover. Clear scope and organised files help keep the project practical for your team.

Questions About Animated Video

How long should our video be?

The right duration depends on the message and viewing context. We recommend a scope after reviewing the brief rather than adding length without purpose.

Can we change the script after animation starts?

Late script changes can affect completed scenes, timing and audio. Additional production and schedule changes are agreed before that work proceeds.

Can you provide voice-over and music?

These can be scoped separately with language, voice style, usage rights and licensing agreed before production.

Ready to plan your animated video project?

Tell us your objective, intended use and timeline so we can recommend a suitable scope.
