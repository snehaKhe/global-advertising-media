PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Your Privacy, Protected.

Trust starts with transparency. Discover how Global Advertising Media handles personal information when you visit our website or get in touch with our team.

Privacy Policy Last updated October 19, 2026
OUR COMMITMENT

Your trust matters to us.

Global Advertising Media ("we", "our", or "us") respects privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and retained when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

The information we process depends on how you interact with us and the features available on our website. Additional notices or terms may apply to specific services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company details, project requirements, and information you choose to provide. Basic technical data may also be recorded by our hosting systems.

How We Use Information

We may use information to answer inquiries, prepare proposals, communicate about projects, provide requested services, maintain security, improve website performance, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Cookies & Analytics

Cookies or similar technologies may support essential website functions and, where configured, analytics or advertising. The technologies used depend on our actual website setup and your applicable consent choices.

When Information Is Shared

Where applicable, information may be shared with service providers that help operate our website or deliver requested services, or disclosed when legally required. We do not sell personal information, as described in this policy.

Security & Protection

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, accounting, contractual, and recordkeeping requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other actions concerning your personal information. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Third-Party Services

Our website may link to third-party websites or use external services. Those providers may handle information under their own policies and terms. Review their privacy notices when using their services.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for people seeking information about our business and services. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

01 / COLLECTION

Where information comes from

Information may be provided directly by you when you contact us, submit a form, request a quotation, or discuss a potential project. Depending on the website configuration, limited technical information may also be collected automatically.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through general inquiry forms unless it is necessary and the relevant channel is appropriate for that information.

02 / PURPOSE & USE

How we handle your information

We use personal information only for relevant purposes, such as responding to your request, managing business communications, delivering agreed services, maintaining website operations, preventing misuse, and meeting applicable obligations.

Where required, we will identify and rely on an appropriate legal basis for processing, provide additional notices, and obtain consent when applicable law requires it.

03 / DATA LOCATION

International data transfers

Depending on the hosting arrangements and service providers we use, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where cross-border transfers occur, we will apply safeguards required by the laws that govern the relevant processing.

04 / POLICY CHANGES

Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our practices, services, technology, or legal requirements. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the revision date.

Please review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
