WHAT THE WORK ACTUALLY INVOLVES

Our SEO Services, Explained in Detail.

Choose a focused service or a coordinated plan. We agree the pages, deliverables, access and reporting before work begins.

Website SEO Audit Keyword Research & Search Intent On-Page SEO Technical SEO SEO Content Strategy & Writing Local SEO & Business Profiles E-commerce SEO Off-Page SEO & Relevant Outreach Website Redesign & Migration SEO International & Multilingual SEO Analytics, Tracking & SEO Reporting Landing Pages & Enquiry Improvements
SERVICE 01

Website SEO Audit

Understand what is holding your website back before investing in changes. We review the current search presence, important pages, site structure and available analytics to establish a practical starting point.

What we do

  • Check indexing, broken links, duplicate pages and content gaps.
  • Assess mobile usability, page templates and the enquiry journey.
  • Prioritise findings by business impact, effort and dependencies.

What you receive

You receive an audit summary and a prioritised action plan, with responsibilities agreed before implementation.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 02

Keyword Research & Search Intent

We research how people describe your products and services, then connect those searches to the pages that can best answer them. The aim is relevant visitors rather than a long list of unrelated keywords.

What we do

  • Group searches by topic, location and customer intent.
  • Review competitor coverage and gaps in your existing content.
  • Map priority topics to service pages, categories and useful guides.

What you receive

You receive a keyword-to-page map and content priorities aligned with your target audience.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 03

On-Page SEO

Make each important page easier to understand for visitors and search engines. We refine the page message while preserving the information customers need to make a decision.

What we do

  • Improve titles, meta descriptions, headings and page copy.
  • Add relevant internal links and descriptive image alternatives.
  • Review page structure, calls to action and overlapping topics.

What you receive

You receive agreed page updates or implementation recommendations, supported by a change log.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 04

Technical SEO

Help search engines access and interpret the right content. We investigate technical barriers and coordinate fixes with your website developer where implementation requires specialist access.

What we do

  • Review crawl access, XML sitemaps, canonical URLs and redirects.
  • Check indexability, status codes and duplicate URL patterns.
  • Assess mobile rendering and opportunities to improve loading performance.

What you receive

You receive a technical issue list, implementation guidance and checks on completed fixes.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 05

SEO Content Strategy & Writing

Create useful content around the questions your customers actually ask. We plan a clear role for each page so your website explains your expertise without repeating the same information everywhere.

What we do

  • Build content briefs, topic plans and an editorial calendar.
  • Write or refresh service pages, guides and frequently asked questions.
  • Check accuracy with your team and agree a review process.

What you receive

You receive approved briefs, drafts or refreshed pages according to the agreed content scope.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 06

Local SEO & Business Profiles

Help nearby customers understand where you operate and what you offer. We focus on accurate business information and useful location content that reflects your real service coverage.

What we do

  • Review Google Business Profile information where eligible and authorised.
  • Check consistency of business name, address and contact information.
  • Improve location pages and plan a genuine customer-review request process.

What you receive

You receive local visibility recommendations and agreed profile or location-page improvements.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 07

E-commerce SEO

Help shoppers discover and compare your products. We review the route from category discovery to product information, with attention to large catalogues and changing stock.

What we do

  • Optimise category and product content, navigation and internal links.
  • Review filters, duplicate URLs, unavailable products and redirects.
  • Recommend relevant product data and structured-data checks.

What you receive

You receive a prioritised catalogue plan and agreed template or page improvements.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 08

Off-Page SEO & Relevant Outreach

Support your online reputation through useful content and relevant relationships. We assess link opportunities for relevance and quality rather than selling a fixed number of links.

What we do

  • Review existing backlinks and relevant industry mentions.
  • Identify appropriate directories, publications and outreach prospects.
  • Develop content ideas that give other sites a reason to reference your business.

What you receive

You receive an outreach plan and an activity record. Publisher acceptance and link placement cannot be guaranteed.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 09

Website Redesign & Migration SEO

Plan for search visibility when moving domains, changing URLs or launching a redesigned website. SEO checks are included in the launch process to reduce avoidable disruption.

What we do

  • Inventory important URLs and map old pages to new destinations.
  • Review redirects, navigation, metadata and indexability before launch.
  • Check the live website and monitor available post-launch search data.

What you receive

You receive a migration checklist, redirect mapping and agreed launch checks; performance can still fluctuate after a move.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 10

International & Multilingual SEO

Structure your website for the countries and languages you genuinely serve. We consider audience needs, language quality and site architecture rather than duplicating the same page for every location.

What we do

  • Review language and country URL structures.
  • Recommend hreflang checks and region-appropriate content planning.
  • Coordinate localisation and translation review with your team.

What you receive

You receive market priorities and technical recommendations; translation and market research are scoped separately when needed.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 11

Analytics, Tracking & SEO Reporting

Understand what changed and what to do next. We connect reporting to your objectives and explain the limits of the available data so decisions are based on useful evidence.

What we do

  • Review Search Console and analytics access where available.
  • Track relevant organic visits, enquiries and completed actions when configured.
  • Summarise completed work, findings and next priorities.

What you receive

You receive an agreed reporting schedule with commentary and recommendations, rather than unexplained ranking tables.

Discuss this service ↗
SERVICE 12

Landing Pages & Enquiry Improvements

Make useful search visits easier to turn into a next step. We review how clearly pages explain your offer and how smoothly visitors can contact you or explore a product.

What we do

  • Review mobile page layouts, contact forms and calls to action.
  • Identify unclear messaging, missing information and unnecessary steps.
  • Recommend focused changes and measurable tests where traffic permits.

What you receive

You receive prioritised page recommendations or agreed updates; testing depends on suitable traffic and tracking.

Discuss this service ↗

Implementation, content quantities, tools, external costs and reporting frequency depend on your proposal. Paid advertising spend is separate from organic SEO work.
